# Engineering Mechanics Study Guide (Semester 1 & 2)

## 1. Equilibrium of Rigid Bodies
- First Axiom: $\Sigma F_x = 0$, $\Sigma F_y = 0$, $\Sigma M_z = 0$.
- Lami's Theorem: For three coplanar concurrent forces in equilibrium:
  $P / \sin(\alpha) = Q / \sin(\beta) = R / \sin(\gamma)$

## 2. Centroid & Center of Gravity
- Rectangle: $x_c = b/2$, $y_c = h/2$
- Triangle: $y_c = h/3$ from the base
- Semicircle of radius R: $y_c = \frac{4R}{3\pi}$ from diameter

## 3. Moment of Inertia
- Parallel Axis Theorem: $I = I_G + A \cdot d^2$
- Polar Moment of Inertia: $J = I_x + I_y$
- Rectangular section: $I_{xx} = \frac{b h^3}{12}$, $I_{yy} = \frac{h b^3}{12}$
- Circular section of diameter D: $I = \frac{\pi D^4}{64}$
